Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to FrameDuo. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how FrameDuo handles information when you use our application.

1. No Data Collection

FrameDuo does not collect any personal data.

We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, rent, share, or analyze your personal information. FrameDuo does not require an account, does not use advertising tracking, and does not build user profiles.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, videos, location history, device identifiers, usage analytics, or any other personal data.

2. Camera Access

FrameDuo requests camera access only to provide live preview and video recording features. Camera data is processed locally on your device for recording purposes.

FrameDuo does not collect or upload your camera feed.

3. Microphone Access

FrameDuo requests microphone access only to record audio together with your video. Microphone audio is processed locally on your device as part of the recording you create.

FrameDuo does not collect or upload your microphone audio.

4. Photos Library Access

FrameDuo may request permission to add recorded videos to your Photos library. This permission is used only when you choose to save your recordings.

FrameDuo does not scan, collect, analyze, or upload your Photos library.

5. Local Recording Files

During recording and export, FrameDuo may temporarily create video files inside the app’s local storage area on your device. These files are used only to complete recording, export, saving, retry, or recovery operations.

These local files are not collected or uploaded by FrameDuo.

6. Optional Location Metadata

FrameDuo may request location access only if you choose to enable optional location metadata. When enabled, location data may be saved locally as metadata in the media file you create.

FrameDuo does not collect, transmit, store, or upload your location data. You can disable location permission at any time in iOS Settings.

7. In-App Purchases

FrameDuo offers an optional one-time Pro purchase through the App Store. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit.

FrameDuo does not collect or store your payment information, Apple ID, credit card information, billing address, or purchase history. Purchase processing, payment, refunds, and account management are handled by Apple.

8. No Analytics or Advertising

FrameDuo does not use third-party analytics tools, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or data brokers.

FrameDuo does not track you across apps or websites.

9. No Server Uploads

FrameDuo does not upload your videos, audio, photos, camera feed, microphone input, location data, or app usage data to our servers.

FrameDuo does not operate a user account server and does not maintain a user database.

10. Children’s Privacy

FrameDuo does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Since FrameDuo does not collect personal data, it does not build profiles of children.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Because FrameDuo does not collect personal data, we do not retain personal data on our servers.

Recordings and temporary files created by the app remain on your device or in your Photos library if you choose to save them. You can delete your recordings from your device or Photos library at any time. You can also delete the app to remove its local app data.

12. Apple System Services

FrameDuo uses Apple system services such as Camera, Microphone, Photos, Location Services, and StoreKit to provide app functionality. These services are provided by Apple and may be subject to Apple’s own privacy policy and terms.

FrameDuo itself does not collect data through these services.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date.

14. Contact Us
