Anything 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月

Anything（以下简称“本应用”或“我们”）是一款个人物品与位置管理工具。本应用不收集任何用户数据。您创建、保存和管理的所有数据仅存储在您的设备以及您自己的私人 iCloud 中，Anything 无法访问这些数据。

1. 数据存储与绝对隐私

库存、位置、标签、备注、条码、照片和其他内容只保存在用户自己的设备与私人 iCloud 中。我们不收集、不上传、不读取、不分析、不出售任何用户数据，也不使用用户数据进行广告或画像。本应用不包含广告。

2. 照片、相机、扫描与 NFC

当您授权相机、照片库或 NFC 后，相关数据仅用于在您的设备上拍摄物品照片、识别条码、包装文字或读取 NFC 标签。OCR、条码识别和图像压缩均在设备上完成，任何原始照片或识别结果都不会离开您的设备或私人 iCloud。

3. iCloud / CloudKit 同步

如果您启用 iCloud 同步，数据只会通过 Apple CloudKit 私有数据库同步到您使用同一 Apple 账户的设备。该私人空间属于您本人，Anything 不拥有访问权限，也不会收集或读取其中的数据。

4. Mac 与 iPhone 协同

协同功能仅在您主动开启并连接设备时工作。扫描结果、物品信息和您主动传输的照片可能通过 Apple 平台提供的设备间连接传输。协同功能不是公开云端服务，也不会用于广告或用户画像。

5. Anything Pro 与 Apple 内购

Anything Pro 是一次性非消耗性购买。购买、恢复购买和权益验证由 Apple App Store 和 StoreKit 处理。我们不会获取您的 Apple ID 密码、姓名或完整支付信息。Apple 可能向本应用提供匿名的交易验证结果，以判断您是否拥有 Pro 权益。

6. 数据导入、导出与删除

导入和导出文件由您主动选择。导出的文件可能包含您选择的库存和位置数据，请妥善保管。您可以在应用内删除数据；删除应用或关闭 iCloud 后，系统仍可能依据 Apple 的备份和同步机制保留一段时间。

7. 系统服务

本应用调用 Apple 提供的系统能力，包括 StoreKit、CloudKit、相机、照片库、地图或通知服务。Anything 不会从这些系统服务收集用户数据。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用不会收集任何用户个人信息，包括儿童个人信息。

9. 政策变更

我们可能更新本政策。更新后的内容将在本页面发布，并以页面所示日期为准。

10. 联系我们

如有隐私问题，请联系：remember9102@163.com。

Anything Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Anything (the “App” or “we”) is a personal inventory and location management app. The App collects no user data. All data you create, save or manage is stored only on your devices and in your own private iCloud, which Anything cannot access.

1. Data storage and complete privacy

Your inventory, locations, tags, notes, barcodes, photos and other content remain only on your devices and in your private iCloud. We do not collect, upload, read, analyze or sell user data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. The App contains no advertising.

2. Photos, camera, scanning and NFC

With your permission, camera, photo library and NFC access is used only on your device to capture item photos, recognize barcodes, read packaging text or associate NFC tags. OCR, barcode recognition and image compression are performed on-device. Original photos and recognition results never leave your devices or private iCloud.

3. iCloud / CloudKit sync

If enabled, your data is synchronized only through Apple CloudKit’s private database to devices using the same Apple Account. This private space belongs to you. Anything has no access to it and does not collect or read its contents.

4. Mac–iPhone collaboration

Collaboration works only when you actively enable it and connect devices. Scan results, item information and photos you choose to send may be transferred through Apple platform connectivity. It is not a public cloud service and is not used for advertising or profiling.

5. Anything Pro and Apple in-app purchase

Anything Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase. Apple App Store and StoreKit handle purchase, restoration and entitlement verification. We do not receive your Apple Account password, name or full payment information. Apple may provide an anonymous transaction verification result to determine Pro access.

6. Import, export and deletion

Import and export files are selected by you. Exported files may contain the inventory and location data you select; keep them secure. You can delete data in the App. After deletion or disabling iCloud, Apple’s backup and synchronization systems may retain data for a limited period.

7. System services

The App calls Apple system capabilities such as StoreKit, CloudKit, camera, photo library, maps and notifications. Anything does not collect user data from these system services.

8. Children’s privacy

The App does not collect personal information from any user, including children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The updated version will be posted on this page and identified by its date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact remember9102@163.com.
